Everyone has a part to play

One team, three ways to make a difference

Simple tools help every person focus on what matters: showing up for students and schools.

01

Coordinator

Keep the whole volunteer program moving smoothly from one place.

  • Manage schools and volunteer clearances
  • Create and staff volunteer shifts
  • View service reports across schools
02

Teacher

Find the extra support you need for classroom activities and events.

  • Request volunteer help for your school
  • Confirm volunteer attendance
  • Help ensure service hours are recorded
03

Volunteer

Put your time and talents to work in a welcoming school community.

  • Find shifts at schools you’re cleared for
  • Join a waitlist when a shift is full
  • Track your volunteer service hours

Clear steps, meaningful impact

A little coordination goes a long way

From a classroom request to confirmed service hours, everyone stays on the same page.

  1. 01

  2. 02

  3. 03
